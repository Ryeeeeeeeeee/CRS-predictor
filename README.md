Mini User Guide for the CRS Risk Prediction Tool

The web-based CRS prediction tool is designed as a clinical decision-support interface that allows rapid estimation of CRS type 2 risk in patients with chronic heart failure using routinely available clinical variables. To use the tool, clinicians enter the five required predictors (age, BUN, BNP, cTNI, and hypertension status) into the input fields. After submission, the system generates an individualized CRS risk probability along with a SHAP-based force plot that visually displays how each predictor contributes to the final estimate.

In practical settings, the tool is intended to support early risk stratification rather than replace clinical judgment. The output should be interpreted in conjunction with patient history, imaging findings, and laboratory trends. Patients identified as high risk may benefit from closer renal monitoring, optimization of hemodynamic management, and multidisciplinary care coordination.

The current version of the tool is research-oriented and has not yet undergone prospective validation in real-time clinical workflows. Future studies will focus on usability testing, electronic medical record integration, and assessment of clinical impact in prospective cohorts.
